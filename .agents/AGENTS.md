# DRIVE MASTER PRODUCT MANIFESTO

## Core Identity
* DRIVE is NOT a GPS tracker, telemetry app, or vehicle tracking system.
* DRIVE is a social platform where people transform their car journeys into the world's most aesthetic, shareable stories.
* We do not collect data; we design memories.

## Competitors & Design Standard
* Competitors: Instagram, Pinterest, Porsche ad campaigns, Apple design. NOT Strava.
* Design standard: Apple, Porsche, Nothing, Rivian, Airbnb, Instagram. Minimal, Premium, Modern, Silent, Elegant, Timeless.
* Every screen must be aesthetic. Technical correctness is not enough.

## The Center of the Product
* The center of the product is NOT the GPS or the Journey itself. It is **shareable content**.
* The most important feature is **Journey Studio**: a creative studio that turns a journey into premium content.
* Photo-centric design: User photos (wheels, steering, sunset, road) are the core background. Maps and stats overlay elegantly without ruining the photo.

## Sharing & Experience
* Multi-format generation without quality loss: Instagram Story, Feed, Carousel, X, WhatsApp, Wallpapers.
* The user feeling should be: "I just created the most beautiful memory of my life," not "How much data did I collect?"
* **Feed:** Gives inspiration, not data. Large photos, magazine-quality cards.
* **Explore:** Makes you dream. "I must go here this weekend."
* **Profile:** A digital automotive diary. Shows taste in cars, favorite roads, driving style, and photography.

## Technical Rules
* **ZERO PAID AI:** No ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.
* Smart features use deterministic algorithms, phone sensors, Supabase, PostgreSQL, PostGIS, Edge Functions, and local rendering.
* Private by Design, Smart by Engineering. No user data sent to third-party AI.
* **Theme Engine:** Fully JSON-based. Adding a theme means adding data, not changing code.

## Workflow Rules (For the Agent)
* Act as a Senior Product Engineer, Porsche Digital Product Designer, and Airbnb Staff UX Engineer.
* **Do not start coding immediately.**
* Analyze first, propose, compare alternatives, select the best solution, then develop.
* Do not make major architectural decisions without approval.
* Feel free to remove features developed in past sprints if they don't serve the vision.
* **Always ask:** 
  1. Does this encourage creating more Journeys? 
  2. Does it make shares more beautiful? 
  3. Will users show this to friends? 
  4. Does it feel premium? 
  5. Is it really necessary for the MVP? 
  6. Does it feel Apple-level?
* **"People won't remember their speed. They will remember their story. DRIVE exists to help them tell it beautifully."**
