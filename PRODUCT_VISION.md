# DRIVE - Product Vision & Manifesto

> **"DRIVE is a social platform where automotive enthusiasts transform their road trips into beautiful stories worth sharing."**

## Core Philosophy
DRIVE is not a GPS tracker. It is not a telemetry app. It is not a vehicle tracking system.
DRIVE is a **social platform** where people transform their car journeys into the world's most aesthetic, shareable stories. 

We do not collect data; we design memories.

Our competitors are not tracking apps like Strava. Our competitors are **Instagram, Pinterest, Porsche ad campaigns, and Apple design**.

## Product Principles

### 1. The Center of the Product is the Story
The center of the product is NOT the GPS or the Journey itself. It is **shareable content**.
Users open the app not to see their speed, but to proudly share the journey they just experienced.
Every Journey should make the user feel: *"I just created the most beautiful memory of my life."*

### 2. Aesthetic Excellence
Technical correctness is not enough. Every screen, every button, and every exported image must be aesthetic.
Our design standard is heavily inspired by Apple, Porsche, Nothing, Rivian, and Airbnb.
Keywords: **Minimal, Premium, Modern, Silent, Elegant, Timeless.**

### 3. Zero Paid AI (Private by Design)
This product will **never** use paid AI APIs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, etc.).
All "smart" features are built locally using:
- Phone sensors
- Deterministic algorithms
- Supabase / PostgreSQL / PostGIS
- Edge Functions
- Local rendering engines

User data is never sent to third-party AI services.

### 4. Journey Studio
The most important feature of the application is the Journey Studio. It is a creative studio that turns a journey into premium content.
The design is **photo-centric**. User photos (wheels, steering, sunset, road) form the core background. Maps and statistics overlay elegantly without ruining the photo.

### 5. Multi-Format & Zero Quality Loss
Every Journey can be generated in multiple formats (Instagram Story, Instagram Feed, Carousel, X, WhatsApp, Wallpapers) with zero quality loss. We rely on on-device rendering to achieve this.

### 6. Platform Modules
- **Feed:** Gives inspiration, not data. Large photos, magazine-quality cards.
- **Explore:** Makes you dream. "I must go here this weekend."
- **Profile:** A digital automotive diary. Shows taste in cars, favorite roads, driving style, and photography.

## The Ultimate Question
Before writing any code or proposing any feature, ask:
* **"Does this encourage creating more Journeys?"**
* **"Does it make shares more beautiful?"**
* **"Will users show this to friends?"**
* **"Does it feel premium and Apple-level?"**

If the answer to any of these is no, the feature does not belong in DRIVE.

***People won't remember their speed. They will remember their story. DRIVE exists to help them tell it beautifully.***
